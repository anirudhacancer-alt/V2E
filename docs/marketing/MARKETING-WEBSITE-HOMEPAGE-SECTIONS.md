## Section H - FAQ (starter)

Q: Is this three different products?  
A: No. It is one platform with three launch packs on a shared execution model.

Q: Which launch pack is most mature first?  
A: Factory Execution is the first commercial wedge; Construction and Field Sales/Retail launch in parallel.

Q: Do we need to replace our current systems?  
A: No. You can start with frontline capture and execution coordination, then add integrations over time.

Q: Is this a Scrum tool?  
A: Internally the model uses agile execution principles, but the product surface is operational: daily plans, handovers, action boards, blocker reviews, and improvement loops.

## 4) Tone and language guardrails
